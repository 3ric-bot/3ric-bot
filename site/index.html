A complete blueprint from marketing to operations

Bring product, curriculum, community, and compliance together. The LearnZo blueprint turns a backlog of specs into a buildable roadmap.

Information architecture
  • Marketing routes for paths, pricing, blog, and comparisons.
  • App shell for students, mentors, admins, and enterprise clients.
  • Dedicated areas for cheatsheets, anatomy, micro-videos, and projects.
Design system & UX
  • Color & typography tokens tuned for dark mode accessibility.
  • Reusable components covering cards, tables, editors, tabs, and alerts.
  • Responsive layout with keyboard-first navigation and skip links.
Content & data models
  • CMS schemas for paths, courses, modules, lessons, assignments, and projects.
  • Extensions for cheatsheets, anatomy entries, micro-videos, and pseudocode drafts.
  • Database tables for progress, submissions, reviews, gamification, and community.
APIs & integrations
  • Auth flows with OAuth, magic link, RBAC, and ABAC controls.
  • Progress, grader, billing, community, analytics, and feature-flag endpoints.
  • Enterprise support for calendar sync, LTI/SCORM, SSO, SCIM, and affiliates.
Operations & security
  • CI/CD pipelines, observability, and DR plans (RPO 1h / RTO 4h).
  • Privacy-by-design, DPIA workflows, ISO 27001 readiness, and audit logging.
  • Runbooks, incident management, and quarterly transparency reports.
Growth & career enablement
  • SEO & content strategy, campaigns, and partner/affiliate operations.
  • Career hub with portfolios, Open Badges, hiring pipelines, and employer dashboards.
  • Telemetry that maps engagement to learning impact and revenue.

A learner experience designed to delight

Deliver modern, motivating UX across web and mobile. Adaptive content blends real projects, hints, and visuals so learners stay in flow.

Interactive practice

Pseudocode generator, sandbox grader, and contextual hints mapped to failed tests. Autosave and offline-ready theory panels.

Community & reputation

Thread templates per lesson, helper badges, mentor queues with SLAs, and moderation guardrails to keep conversations healthy.

Calendar & streaks

Sync with Google, Apple, or Outlook. Smart reminders, streak freeze planning, and analytics-backed pacing suggestions.
